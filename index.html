Tech Career Transition & Market Demand

Shifting to Tech Metrics

  • Income Growth: Career changers entering tech analytics experience an average salary increase of +30% to +45% over prior non-tech roles.
  • NSS Proven Track Record: NSS boasts an overall placement rate of 87% (87.5% Full-Time, 78.4% Part-Time), with over 2,807+ graduates and 600+ Data Analysts placed.
  • Flexibility & Remote Work: Over 68% of data roles offer hybrid or fully remote schedules.

Job Market Demand (BLS 2026)

  • Rapid Growth: According to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS), Data Analyst and Data Science fields are projected to grow +23% through 2032.
  • Volume: Over 150,000+ job openings annually across healthcare, finance, logistics, and tech sectors.
  • Local Hubs: Nashville surges as a healthcare analytics powerhouse anchored by HCA Healthcare, VUMC, Asurion, and Amazon.
